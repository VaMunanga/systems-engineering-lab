## 3. The Rules of the Grid

### 1. Protect the Maker's Block (Saturday)
*   **Sterile Cockpit [936]:** Turn off your phone's Wi-Fi. Close all browser tabs that are not documentation or your terminal.
*   **No "Vibe Coding" [299]:** Do not write code while guessing. If you hit a bug with the WhatsApp Flow payload, open Meta's schema documentation, read it, trace the variables manually on paper, and write the test script. 

### 2. Guard the Hardware [917-918]
*   **Do not drop the workouts.** Gym sessions are limited to **30 minutes maximum** (e.g., heavy squats/deadlifts or bodyweight routine) three times a week. It is an investment in your brain's performance, not a time-sink.
*   **GIGO (Garbage In, Garbage Out) [1169]:** Drink water throughout the day. Avoid late-night sugar crashes.

### 3. Defer the Reading List [986]
*   Put *The Pragmatic Programmer* back on the shelf. You are currently in the **"Exploitation"** phase [1212] of your learning curve. You are writing production-level async database calls—you are living the book. Read it during the semester break.

### 4. Play Backyard Football [600]
*   Stop looking at other students' hackathons. Your friends are playing in a sandbox; you are building an urban logistics fabric in a highly cash-constrained, low-bandwidth economy. Keep your tech stack dirt simple.
